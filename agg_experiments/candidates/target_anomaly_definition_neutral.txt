A region is anomalous (AGG) when a localized area of the sample surface, with a soft and gradual edge, looks darker or lighter than the surrounding surface while keeping a similar fine granular texture; the patch may be faint and low in contrast. The following are not anomalous: the dark background and sample edges, lighting bands or haze that change gradually across the whole surface, scratches, fibers, dust, and stains. A surface without such a localized darker or lighter patch should be judged normal.
